SMS Consent Disclosure

Consent to Receive Appointment Reminder Text Messages

By providing your mobile number during scheduling, booking, or a live phone conversation, you agree to receive transactional text messages related to your appointment or scheduling activity.

What messages you may receive

  • Appointment confirmations
  • Appointment reminders
  • Reschedule or cancellation notices
  • Scheduling follow-ups

How consent is collected

Users provide consent verbally during a call or while booking an appointment. During that process, users provide their phone number and are informed that they may receive appointment reminders and scheduling-related text messages.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on appointment activity. Most users receive only the messages needed to manage their scheduled appointment.

Opt out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, no further text reminders will be sent unless you opt in again.

Help

For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@example.com.

Not marketing

These text messages are used for informational and scheduling purposes only. No promotional or marketing messages are sent through this reminder flow.
